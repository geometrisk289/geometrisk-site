What we believe

A risk decision should start with your appetite — not a product.

Geometrisk exists to flip the order of the risk conversation.

The usual order

Most risk conversations start with a product. Here’s a policy — do you want it? They begin with what an insurer has to sell, not with what the business can actually carry. The most important question gets skipped.

The right order

Start instead with your risk appetite: the loss your business can absorb, the loss it can’t, and what a loss would really cost to recover. Answer that first, and everything after it — what to retain, what to transfer, what to spend — gets clearer.
